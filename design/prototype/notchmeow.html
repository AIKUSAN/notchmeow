Desktop

The big cat out of the notch, living on the desktop. Drag it out of the notch and drop it on the desktop, or use Cat on the desktop. It flies into the notch for an approval or a question and out again once that is answered; its spot in the notch stays empty while it is out. Click opens the island, double-click sends it home, drag moves it, right-click opens its menu. The mock screen is 960 by 540 points, with a menu bar of 32 and a Dock of 64. Every rule is CatCore's: desktopStep, desktopFlightAt, desktopFps, clampSpot, spotFor, rememberSpot and desktopKittenSlots, pinned by desktop-cases.json.

Greeting

The launch greeting, 6.5 s: a cat version of upstream's Greeting v2. The cat falls in with a landing squash, bounces, slides aside, pops up for a quick paw wave, then slides back and settles with the badge and tint. Once the island has folded, the session cats pop in as a row of three, hold a moment and glide into the 2×2 grid the compact island shows, where a fourth cat pops in. The timeline is defined in cat-core.js and pinned by sequence-cases.json, which the apps are tested against.

Size ladder

Below Ø28 the cats switch to the compact drawing: small coloured eyes (an iris in the coat's colours, a slit pupil, a catchlight and a thin rim; the expression shapes stay ink), no stripes or whiskers, flat fills instead of gradients and smaller ears (0.8 times at Ø20 and below, easing back to full size at Ø28). Kittens keep their parent's coat with a rounder head, bigger eyes and ears, and a shorter muzzle.

Small eyes

Every coat and a kitten of it, at the compact sizes, with the ink pills they replace (left of each pair) beside the small coloured eyes (right). The first sheet is magnified 2 times, as the compact drawing at twice the size; the second is true size. Geometry: CatCore.smallEye, pinned by small-eye-cases.json.

Collar

The colour that used to fill the upstream figure becomes a thin collar, in one of three styles, or none (the default). Small cats wear their agent's collar; the big cat wears the collar of the agent driving its state, and none when idle. The coat is never recoloured. Drawn in the front pass, from Ø12 up (the Ø11 session cats on this page wear it too). Pick a style and every cat on this page wears it: the stage cats and the notch cat while they are not idle, the session cats, and the size ladder always. The colours are the real ones from the pill catalogue.
