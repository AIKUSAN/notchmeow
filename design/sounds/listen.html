    Recommended packs

    One cat and one microphone per pack, for a set that sounds consistent.

    1. BigSoundBank (Joseph Sardin)

    Every page says "CC0 (public domain)", and the recordings are professional. "Little meow of a cat #2–#11" is the cat Emi; the s1881–s1902 session has meows, growls and roars; plus toy squeaks #1–#8. All adult cats, no trills.

    2. steffcaffrey, pack "Cats"

    Twelve clips of one male cat. Best for greet, love, question, proud, slap, wink and finish. Open the pack ↗

    3. Department64, the cat Oscar

    Each clip is labelled with an emotion. Take only the kitten## files and Buddha Purr: the same pack holds 2020 CC BY uploads. Open the pack ↗

    4. MBPL, pack "Animals"

    Six soft trills from one cat. Open the pack ↗

    5. Sadiquecat

    Two cats, Pearl and Perran: meows, mrrms, snores and licking, plus dozens of collar-bell (grelot) recordings.

    6. Ticks fallback

    Mafon2's CC0 Freesound clips, and Kenney's Interface Sounds, UI Audio and Impact Sounds (CC0, not cat-themed). Mafon2's "Scaredy Cat" library is sold on Sonniss and is not CC0.

    Suggested core

    • steffcaffrey for chirps
    • Oscar for emotional meows
    • Emi as a cleaner meow voice
    • MBPL for trills
    • Sadiquecat for snores, licks and bells

    Apply one pitch shift to the whole set so it sounds like one cat.
