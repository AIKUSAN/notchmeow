## Packs with one cat and one microphone (for a consistent set)

1. **BigSoundBank (Joseph Sardin).** Every page says "CC0 (public domain)", and the recordings are professional.
   - "Little meow of a cat #2–#11": the cat Emi.
   - The s1881–s1902 session: meows, growls and roars.
   - Toy squeaks #1–#8.
   - All adult cats; no trills.
2. **steffcaffrey, pack "Cats"** (https://freesound.org/people/steffcaffrey/packs/16113/). Twelve clips of one male cat. Best for greet, love, question, proud, slap, wink and finish.
3. **Department64, the cat Oscar** (https://freesound.org/people/Department64/packs/4120/). Each clip is labelled with an emotion. **Take only the kitten## files and Buddha Purr: the same pack holds 2020 CC BY uploads.**
4. **MBPL, pack "Animals"** (https://freesound.org/people/MBPL/packs/37289/). Six soft trills from one cat.
5. **Sadiquecat.** Two cats, Pearl and Perran: meows, mrrms, snores, licking, plus dozens of collar-bell (grelot) recordings.
6. **Ticks fallback:** Mafon2's CC0 Freesound clips, and Kenney's Interface Sounds, UI Audio and Impact Sounds (CC0, not cat-themed). Mafon2's "Scaredy Cat" library is sold on Sonniss and is **not** CC0.

Suggested core:
- steffcaffrey for chirps;
- Oscar for emotional meows;
- Emi as a cleaner meow voice;
- MBPL for trills;
- Sadiquecat for snores, licks and bells.

Apply one pitch shift to the whole set so it sounds like one cat.

Other CC0 sources:
- **Wikimedia Commons:** "Maullido de gata hembra joven.ogg" and "Meow of a Siamese cat - freemaster2.wav". Its purrs there are marked "Public domain", which isn't the same as CC0.
- **OpenGameArt:** kerzoven "Cat Purr & Meow" (https://opengameart.org/content/cat-purr-meow), including sleepy and active purr loops.

## Processing

- **Loudness:** match vocals by short-term loudness at about −20 LUFS. Integrated LUFS is unreliable under about 1 s.
- **Peaks and ticks:** true peak at or below −1 dBTP; ticks sit 6–10 dB below the vocals.
- **Edges:** fades of 2–5 ms in and 10–30 ms out; high-pass at 80–120 Hz.
- **Cleanup:** tame 2–5 kHz on hisses; sum to mono; downsample the 96/192 kHz sources.
- **Kittenify:** shift up 1–3 semitones, using the same shift across the whole set.
- **Loops:** cut at zero crossings with a 50–200 ms crossfade; ship as WAV or CAF, not MP3.
- **Credits:** credit uploaders even though CC0 doesn't require it. Some ask for it: Breviceps, and steprock asks you to credit the cat "Millie".
- **Licence manifest:** keep one with a page archive, because some uploaders mix CC0 and CC BY.
- **Licence file:** `LICENSE-ASSETS.md:12` must exclude the CC0 originals from "all rights reserved".
