NotchMeow sound sets

Five complete sets of the 29 sounds. They differ only in the cat's voice; the ticks, yawn, work, gulp and hairball are the same in all five (search is made in code in three sets, a recorded chatter in Best of and a recorded trill in Tabby). Listen, then pick a version for each cat moment: your picks ship as Your mix.

sound files are missing. Build the sets, then reload this page:

python3 tools/sounds/build_pack.py --all --report

Each cat moment is the cat sound plus a short touch in its mood, and some also get the signature (collar-bell sparkle). Ticks get neither, and neither does the hairball, which is real coughs plus a retch and plop made in code. The per-cat voice (pitch offset) and the chattiness volume are applied in the app at play time, so they are not in these files; the hairball plays at normal speed for every cat, so its retch stays on the beat.

Pick a version for each of the 15 cat moments with the dot beside its Play button, or None of these if nothing fits: it gets a second round, and Alt columns appear on that row once the alternatives are built. Use this set for all fills every row from one set. The other 14 rows are the same in every set, so there is nothing to pick. At 15 of 15, Lock in shows your mix. "Play the whole set" and "Play my mix" run through all the sounds with a short gap. Sounds play from .claude/sounds/build/, which is not in git.
