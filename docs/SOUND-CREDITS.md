The mixes built from them, and the sounds made in code, are NotchMeow's own and stay under `LICENSE-ASSETS.md`.

This file is written from `design/sounds/sources.json` by `python3 tools/sounds/originals.py credits`. Do not edit it by hand: `tests/test_sound_shipped.py` fails when the two differ.
