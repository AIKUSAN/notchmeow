## The CC0 source clips

The recorded clips behind NotchMeow's sounds were released by their makers under Creative Commons Zero
([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)). They stay CC0, and they are excluded from the "all
rights reserved" above: nothing here takes that back. Every recorded clip was decoded from its Freesound HQ preview (MP3); no
original WAV was downloaded. Four sounds and part of the hairball are made in code. Each clip is listed, with its maker, its page, the date its licence was
checked and the SHA-256 of the HQ preview it was built from, in [docs/SOUND-CREDITS.md](docs/SOUND-CREDITS.md) and, for
the build, in `design/sounds/sources.json`. The clips are CC0 and their pages are linked there: use them as CC0 allows.

What stays reserved is NotchMeow's own work on them: how the clips are cut, pitched, layered, levelled and mixed into
the pack's files, and the sounds made in code.
